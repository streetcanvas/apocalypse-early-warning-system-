How it works
01
ADS-B Transponders
Every private aircraft broadcasts its position. ADS-B Exchange aggregates these into heatmaps updated every 30 minutes.
02
FAA Cohort (~31,000 jets)
Business jets and private aircraft tracked via the public FAA registry. Excludes commercial airliners.
03
Baseline Comparison
Current airborne counts compared to a rolling 24h baseline that accounts for time-of-day patterns.
04
Sigma → Level 1–5
Deviation expressed in standard deviations (σ), mapped to emergency levels 1–5.
Level Guide
01 NOMINAL
All clear
02 ELEVATED
Slight uptick
03 WATCH
Significant deviation
04 WARNING
Major spike
05 EMERGENCY
Extreme outlier
⚠ Level 5 can be triggered by holidays, major events, or data artifacts. The history tab lets you audit past anomalies.
